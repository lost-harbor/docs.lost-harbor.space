The opening: Meridian Works to the first jump

Spoilers: this page describes the full opening, including its scripted death and escape.

Start with a new character, not the showcase template. The route below follows the shipped opening walkthrough test. The test uses a Pilot. Combat skills and outcomes differ between classes.

Commands in each block run in order. Type one line at a time and wait for actions to finish. Use look, inventory and jobs whenever you lose your place. A work day advances when you sleep in your bunk, not when you wait in a corridor.

On this page: Intake, first shift, accident, term end, escape, lifeboat, first jump.

Use your class’s available skills instead if needed. Continue fighting until the drone falls. Heal with use stim or use medkit when your hit points run low.

Repeat coax until the drive wakes. All three repairs must succeed before launch: the line, the vanes and the drive.

Launching seals the Works permanently for this character. The game tells you that the way back has closed. Your lifeboat is now in the Verge system.

§First flight and gate jump

Set a course:

Wait until the flight arrives. Then:

Wait for arrival at the gate before typing:

The first gate jump reaches Brindle. The opening is complete. Your character now lives in shared-world space rather than the private Works instance. On the public static site, that world still runs locally in your browser.
