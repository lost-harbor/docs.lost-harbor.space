Crate lh_map 

Expand description

Lays out rooms for the minimap from their exits.

Positions are integer grid cells internally and are reported as f32. Horizontal exits step one cell per compass direction, scaled by their step length. Up and down exits do not move on the grid: they become vertical markers.

layout places only the level of the current room. layout_levels also follows up and down exits. A room above sits on level z + 1 in the same cell as the room below it, or in the nearest free cell of that level. Each level has its own grid, so rooms on different levels never collide.

Functions§

layout
Lays out the rooms on the current level that are reachable from view.here, plus any pinned rooms on that level. Up and down exits become markers.
layout_levels
Lays out every level. Up and down exits still leave a marker on the room they start from, and they also place the room they reach on the level above or below. Rooms pinned to another level are placed there.
