Module carrier 

Expand description

Carriers: hangar bays that hold small craft. Carriers: large ships that hold small craft in a hangar bay.

A carrier is a ship with craft slots (hangar in its record) and a room with the hangar flag. A craft in the bay is berthed there. The berth machinery of the space plugin then does the rest: a berthed ship counts as docked, it leaves the contact list, and the piloting plugin opens its airlock onto the bay, so crews walk between the two ships.

Commands:

  • dock <ship> at a craft helm: lands in the bay of a carrier alongside.
  • recover <ship> at a carrier helm: draws a craft alongside into the bay.
  • launch <ship> at a carrier helm: sends a berthed craft out.
  • launch at a craft helm works as before. The craft leaves the bay and appears where the carrier is.

The rules for a transfer, in check:

  • only small and medium hulls fit a bay,
  • the two ships have the same owner,
  • both hold still in one system, at one place or close together,
  • the bay has a free slot.

Design notes:

  • This plugin adds commands of the same names as the flight plugin at a higher priority. Each handler passes when its words do not name a ship, so the flight commands run unchanged. That is the Chain of Responsibility pattern, and it keeps the flight plugin closed to edits.
  • An observer of Launched moves a craft that leaves a bay to the point where its carrier is. That is the Observer pattern.

Functions§

alongside
True when two ships hold still in one system and close together: at one place, or adrift within NEAR chart units. A ship in a dock or under way is never alongside.
berth_now
Berths craft in bay, the hangar of carrier, and tells both crews.
berthed
The ids of the craft berthed in a carrier’s bay, in id order.
carrier_of_bay
The carrier whose hangar bay room is, or None for any other room.
check
Checks that craft can berth in the bay of carrier now. Returns the bay room, or a message that says why not.
hangar_room
The hangar bay room of a ship, with the owner suffix of a copy. None when the ship has no craft slots or no room with the hangar flag.
