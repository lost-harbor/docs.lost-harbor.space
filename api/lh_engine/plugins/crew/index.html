Module crew 

Expand description

Hirelings: hire, list and dismiss crew who take posts aboard a ship. The crew plugin. A player hires hirelings from an NPC. Each hireling then walks the world as a real entity: it keeps a post aboard a ship, follows its employer, or guards someone. It fights by its stance, and a crew member who dies is gone for good.

It registers:

  • Content kind: hireling, for the builder.
  • Component: Crew on the employer, and Hired on each crew entity.
  • Commands: hire, crew, dismiss, order, assign, and transfer.
  • Presence, verbs, and a looker for crew entities.
  • View: group, with live hit points.
  • System: reconcile, which spawns, moves, and sends crew into fights.
  • Observer: Fell, which removes a dead crew member from the crew.
  • Persistence: crew, which keeps orders, stances, posts, and hit points.

It uses two public seams of other plugins. Combat gives the Combatant component, Vigor, Fighting, and join. Space gives the Ships resource, ship_of_room, and dock_room. Neither plugin knows about crew.
