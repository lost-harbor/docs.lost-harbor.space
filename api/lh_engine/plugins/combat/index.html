Module combat 

Expand description

Combat: real-time rounds, abilities, conditions, death, hazards and loot. The combat plugin: CircleMUD-style real-time fights with 5e resolution.

A fight starts with kill, attack, or hit. Every few seconds a combat round runs, and each fighter attacks its foe: d20 plus an attack bonus against the foe’s armour class. A player can queue one action per round, such as use <ability>, shoot, aim, dodge, disengage, or flee.

Files:

  • mod.rs: the plugin, the components, and shared helpers.
  • rolls.rs: attack rolls, damage, saving throws, and healing.
  • effects.rs: conditions, abilities, consumables, and the effect list.
  • round.rs: the round system, NPC tactics, fleeing, and recovery.
  • death.rs: death, corpses, respawns, XP, and loot.
  • commands.rs: the typed commands and the mover.
  • views.rs: the vitals readout, score, consider, and room lines.
  • hazards.rs: room hazards and behavior actions that hurt or heal.
  • remains.rs: the record that keeps player corpses through a restart.

This plugin registers:

  • Content kind: ability.
  • Commands: kill (aliases attack, hit), shoot, aim, dodge, disengage, flee, use (abilities and consumables), wimpy, consider (alias con), affects (alias aff), assist, abilities (alias powers), and loot. A hidden get handler takes items out of corpses. Hidden rest and sleep handlers refuse during a fight.
  • Mover: hold_in_fight, which stops a fighter from walking away.
  • Presence, verbs, and a looker for corpses and fightable NPCs.
  • Observers: Arrived (aggressive NPCs and hazard warnings), Slept (a long rest clears conditions), GainXp (new abilities), and CombatAction (behavior actions).
  • System: tick, which runs rounds, hazards, and corpse decay.
  • Persistence: wimpy.
  • Records: kind corpse, one record that holds every player corpse.

Independence: fights in other rooms never meet, because every check compares the full room id, and a room in an instance copy carries its owner suffix. Other plugins call a few public hooks here: view, conditions, score_lines, gear_lines, npc_line, and stun_for_ms.

Functions§

attack_roll
Resolves one attack roll from two d20 results. The second die counts only with advantage or disadvantage. A pure function, so tests can check it.
conditions
The condition list for a fighter, as the protocol shows it.
gear_lines
Extra lines for equipment: the attack and armour class the gear gives.
npc_line
The room line of an NPC in a fight, which replaces its usual line. The text follows the NPC’s name, for example “is here, fighting Ash!”.
score_lines
Extra lines for score: armour class, attack, wimpy, and the fight.
stun_for_ms
Applies the stunned condition for about ms milliseconds, and at least one round. Exit guards call it, so their stun is a real condition.
view
The combat part of the vitals view for a player. The round number only counts during a fight, so the view stays still while nothing happens.
