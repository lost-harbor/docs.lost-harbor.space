Module jobs 

Expand description

Jobs from the tally board: accept, deliver and abandon. Jobs from the tally board (on your slate): jobs, accept, deliver, abandon. Delivery jobs complete when you hand over the goods in the right place. Visit jobs when you first reach the place.

A job with steps is a quest: an ordered list of goals, done one at a time (see lh_content::Goal). Its need is ignored. A job with offered_by is only on offer next to one of those NPCs or items, such as a foreman or a terminal, and a job with requires only while those behavior conditions hold, for example a Day range or a flag. A step sets its sets flags when done, and the job sets its own sets flags when the last step is done.

This plugin registers:

  • Content kind: job, so the builder can edit the tally board.
  • Component: Jobs, the player’s active job id and the set of finished job ids. It is saved with the player.
  • Commands: jobs (aliases job and board), accept, deliver, abandon. Also give, a hidden command at priority 5 that finishes a delivery when the player gives the goods in the right room. Otherwise it passes to the ordinary give. Also use, a hidden command at priority 5 for use <item> on <target>. It raises UsedOn and passes any other use to the class features.
  • Fallback: a typed verb that matches the current step’s Check goal rolls the skill check.
  • System: auto_steps in the Update stage finishes Visit, Get and Flag steps as soon as they hold.
  • Observers: on_visit for Visited, which completes visit jobs. on_asked for Asked, on_used for UsedOn and on_defeated for Defeated, which finish Ask, Use and Kill steps. on_start for StartJob and on_advance for AdvanceJob, which let NPC behaviors hand out and finish jobs by content id.
  • View: job (job_view), which shows the active job’s steps and reward.
  • Map marker: job_marker, which shows a job icon in the room where the job is.
  • Persistence: jobs key, using save and load.

Independence: this plugin imports core modules (events, parse, text, world, rules) and the behaviors plugin, whose flags and conditions gate quests and their steps. The rest of the game reaches jobs only through the events and hooks listed above. Completing a job pays XP through the GainXp event, so XP rules stay in one place.
