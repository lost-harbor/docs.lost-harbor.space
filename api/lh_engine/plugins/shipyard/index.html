Module shipyard 

Expand description

Shipyards: buying and selling whole ships, and the fleet list. Shipyards: buying and selling whole ships, and the fleet list.

A character can own many ships, one of each model. Each ship stays where its owner left it. A shipyard is a room that sells owned ship templates. A bought ship waits at the yard’s dock, and the yard buys back any ship of its owner that is docked there, for half the price.

This plugin registers:

  • Content kind: shipyard.
  • Commands: shipyard (alias yard) lists the ships for sale. shipyard buy <ship> and shipyard sell <ship> trade them. fleet lists the ships that the character owns, from anywhere. shipname <name> names the owned ship that the character stands aboard. shipname clear gives it back the product name.
  • The shipyard view, which sends ShipyardView to a character who stands in a yard, or who is aboard a ship docked at the yard’s dock, for the shipyard window.
  • A mover that keeps people out of owned ships that are not theirs. The owner walks in, and the owner’s hirelings follow. When two owners dock the same model side by side, the owner’s keyword leads to their own.

It builds on the space plugin: own_ship makes a new copy and scrap_ship removes a sold one. The shipyard window sends the same typed lines as the commands, so clicking and typing share one path.

Pattern: facade. The commands hide the ship ids, the owner keys and the dock rules behind three short verbs.
