Module traffic 

Expand description

Traffic: NPC ships, the contact list, hail, and transcript traffic lines.

NPC ships come from the traffic lists in system files. Each one is a small state machine: it waits at a stop for dwell seconds, flies to the next stop on its route, and repeats. NPC state lives in memory only. A restart puts every NPC back at its first stop.

Contacts are every ship in a system, player and NPC, in the wire form ShipContact. Contact ids are ship:<id> for player ships and npc:<id> for NPC ships.

Traffic lines keep the transcript of people aboard a ship aware of their neighbours. A ship that is docked or holding at a place hears when another ship departs, arrives, or docks there. A ship also hears when another ship passes within PASS_RANGE. To keep these lines rare, one ship hears at most one line per LINE_GAP seconds, and one pair of ships produces at most one pass line per PASS_COOLDOWN seconds.

Constants§

LINE_GAP
The least number of seconds between two traffic lines to one ship.
PASS_COOLDOWN
Seconds before the same pair of ships produces another pass line.
PASS_RANGE
Ships closer than this, in chart units, count as passing.
