Module phenomena 

Expand description

Phenomena: nebulae, pulsars, black holes, and ion storms, and what they do to ships inside their reach.

Each phenomenon is a place with a reach, a radius in chart units around its position. See Place::reach in lh_content. The effects are:

  • A nebula hides ships. A contact inside one, or flying to a place inside one, does not show on other charts. A ship inside a nebula sees no other contacts and can scan only the nebula and the place where it holds.
  • A pulsar or an ion storm wears the hull of a ship that holds inside its reach. A ship in flight passes through unharmed. The wear stops at HULL_FLOOR, so a ship is never lost to it.
  • A black hole drains fuel from the tank of any ship inside its reach that is not docked, in flight or holding.

repair restores the hull at a dock that sells fuel.

Design pattern: the effects run as one periodic system, a Strategy per phenomenon kind chosen by a match. The contact filter is a Decorator over the plain contact list from traffic.

Structs§

Exposure
The real time of the last effect round for each ship, by ship id. A ship that leaves every reach drops out, so the next entry warns again.

Constants§

DRAIN
Fuel units that one round of black hole pull drains.
EFFECT_SECS
Real seconds between two rounds of hull wear or fuel drain.
HULL_FLOOR
The hull percent below which a pulsar or a storm does no more harm.
REPAIR_PRICE
Credits for each hull percent that repair restores.
WEAR
Hull percent that one round of pulsar or storm wear removes.
