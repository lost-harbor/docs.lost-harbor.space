Module flight 

Expand description

Flight: leaving the dock, flying courses, holding, arriving, and docking.

Commands, all at the helm except status:

  • launch (alias undock): a 3 second action that lifts the ship out of its dock. The ship then holds at the same place.
  • fly <place> (alias course): plans a course with lh_space and burns. From a dock it queues launch first. In flight it replaces the course.
  • hold: cuts the burn. The ship drifts at its current point.
  • dock [place]: docks at the place where the ship holds, in 3 seconds. With another place, it flies there and docks on arrival.
  • status (alias ship): where the ship is, fuel, hull, and drive.

The tick checks every ship in flight. A ship whose tank runs dry switches to reserve thrust and replans from its current point. A ship whose course ends arrives at the target place.
