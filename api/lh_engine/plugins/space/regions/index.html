Module regions 

Expand description

Regions: mine and salvage at a region place, and scan.

A region is a place such as a belt sector. It is not a room. Its actions are listed in the system file, and each one names a verb, an item, a count, and a time.

Design choice: the yield goes into the ship hold, as items on the floor of the room that the ship prototype names as hold (the Wren uses its cargo pod). The pilot stays at the helm, so the ore cannot go to the pilot’s pack. Anyone aboard can walk to the hold and get ore.

scan reads a place or a ship contact. With no word it reads the place where the ship holds, or the nearest contact when adrift.
