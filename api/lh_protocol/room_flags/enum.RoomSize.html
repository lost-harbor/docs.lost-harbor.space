Enum RoomSize 

pub enum RoomSize {
    Tiny,
    Small,
    Medium,
    Large,
    Huge,
    Gargantuan,
}
Expand description

The floor size of a room. Only the ship schematic reads it so far. A bigger room draws as a bigger box, and the hull widens around it.

Variants§

§

Tiny

A crawlspace, a suit locker or a one-person lock.

§

Small

A closet, a lock or a narrow cockpit.

§

Medium

An ordinary room.

§

Large

A big room, such as a hold or a salon.

§

Huge

A very big room, such as the bay of a bulk hauler.

§

Gargantuan

A room that dwarfs the crew, such as a hangar or an ore bunker.
