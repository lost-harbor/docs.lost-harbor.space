Module sheet 

Expand description

The ship sheet: the top view, the side view and the front view of one hull on one drawing, all at one scale.

The top view comes from crate::ui::blueprint and the side and front views from crate::ui::profile. Each view is drawn in its own nested SVG, and the sheet only places them:

  • The top view and the side view share the bow face of the first row of rooms, so a frame in one view lies over the same frame in the other.
  • Every view keeps the centre line of the main level on one height where the views sit side by side.
  • arrange picks one of two layouts. The top view goes over the side view, or to the left of it, whichever lets the ship draw larger in the space the sheet gets. A tie, or a small gain, keeps top over side.

The sheet has two looks. The plain look is the drafting schematic. The drawn look paints the hull in the colours of its style, lit from the top left like the portraits: a lit half and a shaded half, a soft run of tone across each half, fine plate texture and trim stripes. The shapes are the same in both looks. The nav chart is meant to use the drawn top view at close zoom.

Design pattern: Composite. The sheet is one drawing made of views that each also draw alone.
