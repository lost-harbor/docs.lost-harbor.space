Expand description

The SVG defs of a drawn view: the light gradient, the plate texture, the hazard stripes and the clip to the hull.

from and to are the ends of the light, in view box pixels. The lit half runs from from to the middle, and the shaded half from the middle to to. A hard step at the middle gives the two tone look of the portraits, and a soft run on each half keeps the plate from reading flat.
