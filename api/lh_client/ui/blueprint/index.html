Module blueprint 

Expand description

The ship blueprint in the ship panel. Documented in its own file. Public so the generator labs in lh_lab can draw it alone. The ship blueprint: a vector deck plan in the ship panel.

The server sends the rooms of the ship as a small MapView inside the DeckPlan. This module lays it out with lh_map, the same way as the room map, and then drafts a hull around the rooms. Nothing is drawn by hand, so every ship that a builder makes gets a blueprint for free.

The work has two parts:

  • draft is pure geometry. It turns the plan into a Draft in grid units: one sheet per level, the hull outline, the drive, the doors, and the hatches. Tests run it without a browser.
  • Blueprint draws the draft as SVG. It has two modes. The full schematic shows every deck in the true direction of the rooms. A room that the character has visited opens a menu with “Walk here”, which sends walk <room>. The exterior mode shows only the hull, lying on its side with the bow to the right, for the card under the nav chart.

The draft points the bow up. The bow is the side of the cockpit. The drawing then turns the draft for its mode. The look is a working ship, not a rocket: a blocky tower of decks with plate seams, a prow, point defence turrets on the walls, and a drive under a collar at the stern. Ships are chunky workhorses with angular lines, and no part copies a well known ship from fiction. The drive is never a rocket bell. It is a bank of square ports, a stack of field emitters, or a pair of pods bolted to the collar. The hull class picks the details.

Pattern: adapter. The draft adapts the room-map layout to a ship drawing, so the blueprint and the room map always agree on where rooms are.

Functions§

Blueprint
The ship blueprint in one of two modes.
bow_up
Step 1 of the draft: lays out the rooms and turns the grid so the bow points up. It gives the layout, the turned cell and level of each room, and the turn. The side profile starts from the same cells.
chaikin
Rounds a closed outline by Chaikin corner cutting, rounds times.
corner_cuts
The two cuts where a hull wall steps between rows of the top view: (wide, narrow). The wider row loses wide from its corner. The narrower row gets a slope narrow long, which runs into its own empty margin, so no cut ever reaches a room box. hh is half the row length.
draft
Drafts the blueprint of a deck plan. See the module doc.
envelope
The half width of each row of a hull, from the least half width that its rooms need.
exterior_view
Draws the outside of a hull, bow to the right. It adds the bridge windows and the outer ends of the hatches, so the bare hull still reads as a ship. With paint, the hull is drawn in its colours and not drafted.
length_m
The length of the ship from bow to stern, in metres. It spans the hull outline and every drive part in the plan, so a long drive counts.
mount_rows
The rows that carry heavy gun mounts, from the bow back.
wall_x
The x of the hull wall at height y of the top view: the far right crossing when right is true, the far left one otherwise. A slope at a width step can stand outside the row band, so fittings that touch the wall ask the traced outline and not the band. Falls back to or when no edge crosses y.
wall_y
The y of the hull wall at column x of the top view: the stern crossing when aft is true, the bow crossing otherwise. It mirrors wall_x for hatches that open through the bow or the stern. Falls back to or when no edge crosses x.
