Module profile 

Expand description

The side profile of a ship. Documented in its own file. Public so the generator labs in lh_lab can draw it alone. The side profile of a ship: the hull seen from starboard, bow to the right. Seen from starboard the bow lies on the right, so the view is true and not a mirror. The top view puts starboard at the bottom.

The blueprint in crate::ui::blueprint looks down on the decks. This module looks at the same ship from the side. It starts from the same cells, so the two views always agree: a row of rooms in the plan is a column of hull in the profile, and each level is one band of hull.

The work has two parts, like the blueprint:

  • profile is pure geometry. It gives a Profile, a list of Parts in grid units. Tests run it without a browser.
  • HullProfile draws the parts as SVG.

The parts:

  • one outline of hull over every column. A column is one row of rooms in the plan. It runs from its highest level to its lowest, and a slope joins two columns of different heights.
  • the prow, on the main axis, in the shape of the hull style. Every prow is the same above and below its centre line.
  • a porthole for each room, more for a longer room, and an amber outer door on the airlock.
  • the island, the gun mounts and the cranes on the top of the hull.
  • the launch boats in davits high on the side, and the tank pods low.
  • the collar, the open spine with its containers, and the drive.
  • the fin and wings of a racer.

The level with the most floor sets the centre line, as it does in the top view. The collar, the drive, the belt and the pods sit on it.

Design pattern: this is a second Builder over the same plan as the blueprint. One input gives two products.
