Expand description

Combat stats for an NPC. An NPC without them cannot be attacked.

Fields§

§hp: i32

Hit points. Zero means seven per NPC level.

§ac: i16

Armour class. Zero means 10 plus half the NPC level.

§attacks: Vec<NpcAttack>

The attacks. The NPC uses one each round, in turn. Empty gives one strike of 1d6 plus half its level.

§abilities: Vec<String>

Ability ids that the NPC uses, as players do, without cell costs.

§aggression: Aggression

How the NPC behaves toward characters.

§assist: bool

True when the NPC joins a fight against any NPC in its room.

§flee_at: u8

The NPC tries to flee below this percent of its hit points. Zero means never.

§loot: Vec<Loot>

What drops into its corpse.

§xp: u32

The XP for the kill, shared by the fighters. Zero means the 5e value for its level.

§respawn_secs: Option<u32>

Seconds before it respawns at home. None means 300. Zero means never.

§ignore_flag: Option<String>

Aggressive NPCs and drones leave alone any character with this flag.

§save: Option<i32>

The bonus on every saving throw. None means half its level.
