Module alien 

Expand description

Alien portraits: the Thren, and others almost, but not quite, shaped like people.

The proportions are wrong on purpose. The head is tall and narrow, the eyes are large and set low and wide, there is no nose bridge and no ears, and the neck is long. They dress in layers of grey cloth with faded ribbons. Aliens read apart from people in two ways. A pale halo stands behind the head, and every shape has a thin cool edge line.
